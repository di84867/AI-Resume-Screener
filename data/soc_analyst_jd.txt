A Security Operations Center (SOC) Analyst is a cybersecurity professional responsible for monitoring, detecting, investigating, and responding to cyber threats in real-time. Working within a SOC, they use tools like SIEM and EDR to analyze alerts, triage security incidents, and mitigate risks to company data and networks, often working in 24/7 shifts.

Key Responsibilities
Continuous Monitoring: Reviewing SIEM, IDS/IPS, and EDR consoles to identify security alerts and anomalies.
Incident Investigation: Analyzing security alerts to determine if they are true threats or false positives.
Threat Response: Executing incident response protocols, including containing, eradicating, and recovering from threats.
Log Analysis: Examining security logs and network traffic to identify root causes.
Documentation & Reporting: Creating detailed reports on incidents and updating documentation for future prevention.
Continuous Improvement: Staying updated on emerging threats and recommending security tool enhancements.

Technical Knowledge: Familiarity with network protocols, operating systems (Windows/Linux), and security tools.

Certifications: CompTIA Security+, Certified SOC Analyst (CSA), or similar are highly preferred.
Soft Skills: Strong analytical skills, communication, and ability to work in high-pressure, fast-paced environments.
